Class 10 Chemistry – Important Ions

Positive Ions — Cations

  • Hydrogen (H⁺): +1
  • Sodium (Na⁺): +1
  • Potassium (K⁺): +1
  • Silver (Ag⁺): +1
  • Ammonium (NH₄⁺): +1
  • Magnesium (Mg²⁺): +2
  • Calcium (Ca²⁺): +2
  • Zinc (Zn²⁺): +2
  • Copper (Cu²⁺): +2
  • Iron (Fe²⁺): +2
  • Aluminium (Al³⁺): +3
  • Iron (Fe³⁺): +3

Negative Ions — Anions

  • Chloride (Cl⁻): −1
  • Bromide (Br⁻): −1
  • Iodide (I⁻): −1
  • Hydroxide (OH⁻): −1
  • Nitrate (NO₃⁻): −1
  • Hydrogen carbonate (HCO₃⁻): −1
  • Oxide (O²⁻): −2
  • Sulphide (S²⁻): −2
  • Carbonate (CO₃²⁻): −2
  • Sulphate (SO₄²⁻): −2
  • Sulphite (SO₃²⁻): −2
  • Phosphate (PO₄³⁻): −3

Easy Tricks

  • Group 1 metals → +1: Na, K
  • Group 2 metals → +2: Mg, Ca
  • Oxygen family → usually −2: O, S
  • Halogens → −1: Cl, Br, I
  • Aluminium → +3: Al³⁺

First memorize these 10: Na⁺, K⁺, Mg²⁺, Ca²⁺, Al³⁺, Cl⁻, O²⁻, OH⁻, SO₄²⁻, CO₃²⁻
